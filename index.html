OUR STORY

Crafted drop by drop

Every SCENTRA composition begins with a memory rather than a formula. We source French and Swiss concentrates, macerate them for six weeks, and bottle in small batches so nothing leaves the atelier before it is ready.

01

SOURCING

Naturals and premium concentrates from certified houses.

02

BLENDING

Hand-blended in micro batches by our perfumer.

03

MACERATION

Six weeks of rest for depth and longevity.

04

FINISHING

Filled, sealed and boxed by hand in Ahmedabad.
