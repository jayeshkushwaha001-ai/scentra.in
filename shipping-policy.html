Shipping Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At SCENTRA Fragrances, we strive to deliver your orders promptly and safely across the country.

1. Nationwide Delivery (Pan-India)

We provide pan-India shipping across all major states, union territories, and pin codes. No matter where you are located in India, we ensure your favorite fragrances reach your doorstep.

2. Order Processing & Shipping Timelines

  • Order Processing Time: All orders are processed within 1 to 2 business days after verification of payment (UTR verification).
  • Estimated Delivery Time: Standard shipping usually takes 4 to 7 business days depending on your delivery location. Metro cities generally receive shipments faster.

3. Shipping Charges

Shipping fees, if applicable, are calculated at checkout based on your order value or location. Any special free shipping offers will be automatically applied at checkout.

4. Order Tracking

Once your package is dispatched, we will send order confirmation and tracking updates via Email or SMS using the details provided during checkout.

5. Incorrect Address & Undelivered Packages

Please ensure your full name, complete delivery address, contact number, and PIN code are accurate before placing an order. SCENTRA is not liable for delayed or returned shipments resulting from incorrect address details provided at checkout.
