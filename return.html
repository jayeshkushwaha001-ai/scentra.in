Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Strict Policy Notice: All sales made on SCENTRA Fragrances are final. We do not accept returns, exchanges, or issue refunds under any circumstances.

Due to personal hygiene and safety considerations associated with fragrance and cosmetic products, all items purchased from SCENTRA Fragrances are strictly non-returnable, non-exchangeable, and non-refundable once purchased.

2. Order Cancellations

Once an order has been placed and payment/UTR details are submitted, the order cannot be canceled, modified, or refunded.

We ensure that all our fragrance formulations are carefully packed and checked for quality prior to dispatch to prevent any issues during transit.

If you have questions regarding our policy before making a purchase, feel free to reach out to our support team.
