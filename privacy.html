Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At SCENTRA Fragrances, we respect your privacy and value the trust you place in us. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled.

1. Guest Checkout Experience

We operate on a Guest Checkout Model. You do not need to create an account, create a password, or register on our website to make a purchase. We collect only the essential information needed to fulfill and deliver your order.

2. Information We Collect

When placing an order, we collect only the necessary delivery and contact details:

  • Full Name
  • Phone Number
  • Email Address
  • Delivery Address, City, State, and PIN Code
  • Transaction Reference / UTR Number for payment verification

3. Direct UPI Payment Processing

Payments on SCENTRA are made directly via UPI (Unified Payments Interface). We do not store, request, or have access to sensitive financial credentials such as your bank account numbers, PINs, card CVVs, or online banking passwords.

4. Use of Information

The information provided is used strictly to:

  • Verify UPI payment details against the provided UTR/Reference number.
  • Package and ship your order to the specified address.
  • Send order updates and tracking notifications.

5. Data Protection & Sharing

We respect your personal data. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal details to third parties for marketing purposes. Your shipping details are shared only with trusted delivery/courier partners solely for delivery purposes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us through our website contact channels.
